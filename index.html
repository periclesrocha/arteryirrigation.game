Match each region to its artery

Pick an artery on the left, then pick every region it irrigates from the list on the right. You can also pick a region first and then its artery. A few regions get blood from two arteries, so their names show up twice.

How do you want to play?

Your score is your time plus 10 seconds for each mistake and 20 seconds for each hint. Lower is better.
